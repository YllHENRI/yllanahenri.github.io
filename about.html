About me

I’m a UI/UX designer who loves exploring how people interact with technology and finding ways to make those experiences simpler, clearer, and more enjoyable.

What excites me most about UX is the mix of empathy and problem-solving: listening to users, understanding their challenges, and turning that into designs that genuinely help. Every project feels like a puzzle where research, creativity, and iteration come together to make something meaningful.

Looking ahead, I want to keep growing as a designer by working on products that have real impact, collaborating with diverse teams, and contributing to solutions that improve people’s everyday lives.

Skills

UX Research & Strategy

User interviews

Conducting user interviews is one of my favorite parts of the process. Talking directly with users helps me uncover valuable insights and understand their real needs and frustrations.

Usability testing

I really enjoy organizing and running usability tests to see how people interact with designs. It’s always exciting to observe unexpected behaviors and use those findings to make products simpler and more intuitive.

Journey mapping

I create journey maps to visualize the user experience, identify friction points, and reveal opportunities to improve the overall flow.

Personas

I build personas and proto-personas to define user types early in a project. They help align teams around shared goals and keep empathy at the center of every design decision.

Design & Prototyping

Wireframing

I create wireframes to explore structure and layout early in the process. It helps clarify ideas and align the team before focusing on visual details.

UI Design

I design clean and accessible interfaces that combine usability with aesthetics, making sure every element has a purpose.

Design Systems

I enjoy building and maintaining design systems that ensure consistency, scalability, and efficiency across projects.

Interaction design

I focus on crafting intuitive interactions and smooth flows that make digital experiences feel natural and engaging.

Visual Design

Graphic Design

I design digital and print materials such as leaflets, posters, and visuals that communicate ideas clearly and attractively.

Visual Identity

I enjoy creating cohesive visual identities. From defining color palettes and typography to ensuring brand consistency across touchpoints.

Marketing material

I create visuals for social media and other communication channels, aligning them with brand values and enhancing audience engagement.
